# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project management processes summary

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative lifecycle with clear ownership and data-informed decisions. **Initiation** validates the business need, identifies stakeholders and measurable success criteria, and determines whether the work is ready for planning. **Planning** turns approved initiatives into prioritized, estimable backlog items, defines the Definition of Done, and maps milestones, risks, and dependencies.

During **execution**, teams track work on a project board, deliver small increments through tested and reviewed pull requests, and coordinate through standups, delivery reviews, and demos. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks and blockers are monitored and escalated through the agreed path.

For **release**, teams confirm acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, prepare release notes and smoke tests, and document rollback or mitigation plans. After a sprint, release, milestone, or incident, teams hold retrospectives, assign owners and due dates to improvement actions, and follow up on their impact.

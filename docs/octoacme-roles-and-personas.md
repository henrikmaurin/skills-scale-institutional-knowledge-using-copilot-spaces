# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor / Business Owner

### Role Summary
The Project Sponsor or Business Owner defines the business case, priorities, and expected outcomes for the initiative. They provide sponsorship, funding alignment, and decision support so the project remains connected to business value.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, success criteria, and expected outcomes
- Sponsor the initiative and approve scope, funding, and priority trade-offs
- Coordinate with project and product leaders to resolve business issues
- Provide leadership sponsorship for stakeholder alignment and strategic decisions
- Escalate decisions that affect business value, timeline, or risk

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned with business needs
- Support successful delivery of measurable outcomes
- Reduce uncertainty in funding, scope, and stakeholder expectations

### Typical Communication
- Executive briefings and steering committee reviews
- Business case and milestone approvals
- Escalation of strategic decisions and priority changes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager on timing, scope, resource trade-offs, and risk escalation
- Partners with the Product Manager to confirm business value and priority sequencing
- Collaborates with the Technical Lead and Quality Lead on feasibility, risk, and delivery confidence
- Provides direction to stakeholders and ensures adoption outcomes are considered

---

## Product Owner / Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
The Product Owner or Requirements Lead translates stakeholder needs into actionable requirements and priorities. They help the team understand what value is being created and how success will be measured.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain requirements, user needs, and backlog priorities
- Align features and acceptance criteria with business outcomes
- Support decision-making on trade-offs and scope adjustments
- Validate that delivered work meets stated value and quality expectations
- Coordinate feedback from stakeholders with delivery teams

### Goals
- Deliver the right solution for users and stakeholders
- Maintain clear, prioritized requirements and acceptance criteria
- Improve alignment between business needs and delivery work

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and prioritization discussions
- Stakeholder conversations and requirement reviews
- Acceptance criteria validation and release readiness reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with the Project Manager on scope, dependencies, and milestone readiness
- Guides Developers and the Technical Lead on requirement clarity and solution trade-offs
- Partners with the Quality Lead to define validation and acceptance evidence
- Helps the Project Sponsor ensure the product remains aligned with strategic objectives

---

## Technical Lead / Solution Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Solution Architect guides technical direction, architecture decisions, and delivery feasibility. They help keep the project technically sound while balancing business needs, complexity, and delivery speed.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, architectural direction, and key design decisions
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and constraints
- Guide engineering standards, integration patterns, and solution quality
- Partner with project and product leaders on estimates and trade-offs
- Support implementation decisions across teams or systems

### Goals
- Maintain a sound, scalable, and maintainable solution
- Reduce technical risk and delivery surprises
- Balance business urgency with feasible technical execution

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency planning and risk reviews
- Technical trade-off decisions and implementation guidance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on timelines, dependencies, and delivery sequencing
- Advises Developers on design consistency, solution quality, and technical constraints
- Works with the Product Owner on feasibility and prioritization trade-offs
- Collaborates with the Quality Lead to ensure technical quality and release readiness
- Supports the Project Sponsor by clarifying cost, complexity, and risk impacts

---

## Quality / Test Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality or Test Lead defines quality expectations, testing strategy, and evidence of readiness. They help ensure the project delivers reliable outcomes and reduces risk to users and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, test strategy, and validation approach
- Review acceptance criteria and ensure test coverage is sufficient
- Coordinate defect triage, quality review, and release readiness checks
- Partner with product and engineering leaders on quality trade-offs
- Help verify the solution meets usability, reliability, and business expectations

### Goals
- Reduce defects and release risk
- Improve confidence in delivery quality and acceptance
- Ensure quality is visible and measurable throughout the project

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and readiness reviews
- Defect triage, acceptance discussions, and release checks
- Quality metrics and stakeholder updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Owner to align acceptance criteria and validation criteria
- Coordinates with Developers on defect prioritization and remediation
- Provides input to the Project Manager on readiness risks and release dependencies
- Partners with the Technical Lead on system quality, reliability, and validation plans
- Supports the Project Sponsor by assessing readiness and risk to business outcomes

---

## Change and Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
The Change and Adoption Lead prepares users and stakeholders for the change that comes with delivery. They help manage communication, readiness, and adoption so the project delivers value beyond launch.

### Responsibilities
- Define stakeholder communication plans and adoption activities
- Support training, rollout readiness, and behavioral change planning
- Identify risks to user adoption and mitigation actions
- Coordinate with business and delivery stakeholders on engagement activities
- Measure adoption progress and support post-launch feedback

### Goals
- Improve readiness and stakeholder confidence
- Increase adoption and minimize disruption
- Connect delivery outcomes to sustained business value

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and readiness updates
- Training plans and adoption progress reviews
- Feedback loops with users and project sponsors

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager on communications timing, stakeholder engagement, and rollout plans
- Aligns with the Product Owner on user needs, experience expectations, and adoption goals
- Coordinates with the Technical Lead and Quality Lead to ensure the solution is usable and ready for release
- Supports the Project Sponsor in demonstrating value realization and business impact

---

## Operations / Service Owner

### Role Summary
The Operations or Service Owner ensures the solution can be supported, sustained, and operated after delivery. They represent operational readiness, supportability, and long-term accountability.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational requirements and support responsibilities
- Support transition to service ownership and ongoing run processes
- Coordinate monitoring, reliability, support, and incident readiness
- Validate deployment readiness and service handoff criteria
- Ensure continuity between delivery and operational support

### Goals
- Improve service resilience and operational continuity
- Reduce support burden and service disruption
- Support long-term value delivery after launch

### Typical Communication
- Service readiness reviews and operational handoff discussions
- Incident management and support planning
- Operational metrics and post-launch reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with the Technical Lead on system reliability, supportability, and handoff criteria
- Works with the Project Manager on release and transition planning
- Coordinates with the Quality Lead on operational validation and readiness evidence
- Aligns with the Product Owner and Sponsor on sustainability, value delivery, and stakeholder expectations

---

## Role relationships and accountability

These roles work together to strengthen clarity across project leadership, execution, quality, service readiness, and adoption. In smaller teams, one person may take more than one role, but accountability for each responsibility should still be explicit. Clear role ownership reduces overlap, supports faster escalation, and improves coordination across project activities.

Use these personas to shape project scenarios and ensure communication, ownership, and decision-making are visible across the project lifecycle.
